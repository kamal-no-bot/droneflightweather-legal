Pitot reads weather. It does not know your airspace, and it never says a flight is safe, legal, cleared or permitted. You are responsible for your flight, for the rules where you are flying, and for checking your aviation authority's own tool before you take off.

What the app is

Pitot shows forecast weather hour by hour against limits that you set yourself, plus the public planetary K index and the twilight hours. Its verdicts are go, caution and no-go, and they describe the WEATHER against YOUR OWN numbers. They are not an authorisation, a clearance or advice.

What the app is not

  • It is not an airspace tool. It draws no map, no no-fly zone and no airport ring, and it links to the aviation authorities' own tools by plain name.
  • It is not aviation advice, and no verdict it shows is a permission to fly.
  • It reports SURFACE wind, not winds aloft, and a forecast is a forecast.

The forecast comes from other people

Hourly weather is Apple Weather's. The planetary K index is NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center's. Neither is ours, neither is guaranteed by us, and an outage or an error in either shows up in the app. Pitot labels how old a reading is on every screen that draws one.
